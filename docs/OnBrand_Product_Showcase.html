1. Executive Product Overview

OnBrand is an enterprise brand governance and regulatory compliance platform engineered to eliminate marketing compliance violations, brand degradation, and accessibility defects before publication.

Unlike ungrounded generative AI tools that hallucinate claims and fabricate legal citations, OnBrand pairs client-side open-weight LLM models and WASM parsers with a two-pass mechanical verification pipeline and a durable PostgreSQL persistence layer. Every claim quote is mechanically verified against source documents, and every cited fact is proven against approved brand databases with strict cryptographic draft hash integrity.

2. Technical Architecture & Hybrid Engine

Client-Side Open-Weight Intelligence (User-Side Execution)

OnBrand leverages user-side open-weight language models and WASM runtimes (Tesseract.js WASM OCR, local text layer extractors, Mammoth OpenXML AST parsers) running entirely in the client browser. This delivers instant, zero-latency document analysis, bounding-box coordinate mapping, and claim extraction with zero external API dependencies and complete privacy for sensitive pre-market copy.

Zero-Hallucination Mechanical Grounding Gates

Every compliance finding must satisfy two deterministic server-side gates:

  • Verbatim Document Validator: Mechanically verifies character offsets in the draft text. Inventions or paraphrases are immediately rejected.
  • Citation Provenance Gate: Checks that cited evidence exists in active, approved organizational guidelines, is non-revoked, within its effective date window, and matches the publication channel.

Enterprise PostgreSQL Persistence & Object Storage

Backed by PostgreSQL 17 via the Supabase Transaction Pooler (port 6543) and Drizzle ORM. Brand knowledge sources, facts, drafts, compliance reviews, and immutable audit trails persist across serverless cold starts. Durable binary assets (logos, packaging posters, documents) are secured in private Supabase Object Storage (onbrand-assets) with strict cross-tenant isolation.

Bi-directional plugins for Figma, Adobe Creative Cloud, and Canva to perform perceptual logo, palette, and contrast checks directly inside creative tools before asset export.

Phase 2 (Months 3–4): Real-Time Collaborative Compliance Editing

Visual constraint authoring for legal teams to customize jurisdiction-specific rules (FDA 21 CFR §101, FTC Endorsement Guides, EU 1169/2011) per distribution market.
